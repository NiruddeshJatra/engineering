## What this episode covers
- Computing History
- Abacus: first calculating machine.
- Computer: job title of manual computing
- Step Reckoner: 10-teeth gear based computing machine
- Difference Engine: can solve complex equation developed by Charles Babbage (Father of Computing)
- Analytical Engine: General Purpose Engine. Both are developed by Babbage, both are mechanical
- Program: Hypothetical instructions wrote by Ada Lovelace (first programmer) for the analytical engine
- Tabulating Machine: Electro-mechanical, punch card based machine invented by Herman Hollerith
- IBM: company that manufactured these machines, established by Herman and others

## Interesting Things to Know
- Militaries first applied computing to solve complex problems like setting angle for cannon ranges.

## What I understood
This episode covered how computing progressed from hand-computed system to mechanical system. The first generation machines could do simple math operations, next gen could solve complex equations, finally progressing to store data also. Each invention or development made computing faster and easier.
