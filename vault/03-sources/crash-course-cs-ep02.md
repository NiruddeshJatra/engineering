## What this episode covers
- Relay based computers: relay is electronically controlled mechanical switches. Electron flow can be controlled by a wire.
- Vacuum Tube based computers: enabled faster computing than relay based computers
- ENIAC: first general purpose, programmable, electronic computer
- Transistor: switches made with semiconductors. Revolutionized computers

## Interesting Things to Know
- The term "computer bug" gained popularity after a real moth was found trapped in a relay of the Harvard Mark II computer on September 9, 1947, causing a malfunction.
- Both relay based and vacuum tube based computers were so large that a whole office floor was needed for the setup.
- Transistor development mostly happened in Santa Clara Valley. As the core material of transistor is silicon, this region became popular as Silicon Valley.
