## Definition
A component that converts our key strokes to ASCII/Unicode

## Details
- Encoder takes multiple inputs and compress them into a smaller [[Binary Numbers]]. For example, when we press "K" in our keyboard, it turns that specific key-press into a binary code (like `01001011`).

## Why it matters to me as a dev
I always wondered which component does the job of converting letter and characters to binary. Now, it is clear to me.

## In my words
So, ultimately, each key in the keyboard is connected to an encoder in a way that whenever we type a key, it gets converted to binary by encoder and gets stored in [[RAM]]
