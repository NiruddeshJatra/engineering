## Definition
Algebra written by George Boole based on logic

## Details
- It has only two values or variables: True(1), False(0)
- It uses these two binary states for all its operations and building logics
- It has three basic operations: AND, NOT, OR
- AND is True when both of its inputs are True
- OR is True when any of its inputs are True
- NOT is True when input is False

## Why it matters to me as a dev
In programming, we use conditional statements almost everywhere and it resembles Boolean Logic
