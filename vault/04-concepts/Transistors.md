## Definition
semiconductor based chip

## Details
- Can work like a switch just like relay or vacuum tubes
- Smaller, faster and more reliable
- A transistor is made of three parts: Emitter, Base, Collector
- Base is the thinnest layer of all and very little doped
- A little current flow to the base section starts the main current flow
- IBM 608 was the world's first transistor based commercially used computer.
- William Shockley established Intel, world's largest chip (semiconductor) manufacturer.

## Why it matters to me as a dev
Transistor revolutionized modern computers, then economy and information network boomed
