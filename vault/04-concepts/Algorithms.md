## Definition
The steps a program use to solve a problem

## Details
- **The Black Box Model:** Computer Science is essentially the study of problem-solving. It uses a model where **Input** (the problem) enters a **Black Box** (the algorithm), resulting in **Output** (the solution).
- **The Role of Abstraction:** This is the most important concept for a developer with your experience. We "stand on the shoulders of others" by using high-level tools (like Python or AI APIs) that hide (abstract away) the complex low-level details of binary and hardware.
- **AI as a "Co-Pilot":** While AI helps solve the "human bottleneck" of limited time and team size, you remain the "pilot" or "conductor". Understanding fundamentals ensures you can direct the AI effectively and fix its mistakes.
- - **Linear vs. Binary Search:**
    - **Linear Search:** Checking one by one; it is slow (takes _n_ steps for _n_ items).
    - **Binary Search:** Tearing the problem in half repeatedly. This is **logarithmic**, meaning even if the data doubles, it only adds one extra step to the search.
- **Engineering Quality:** Software should be **Correct** (works as intended) and **Well-Designed** (uses the least CPU, RAM, and human time).
- **The Corner Case:** Professional code must handle "else" scenarios, like what to do if a search result isn't found. Failing to handle these cases is why software "freezes" or reboots.
 
## Why it matters to me as a dev
For a developer, the most important takeaway is **Abstraction**. We use high-level tools—like the OpenAI API or Python—to solve complex problems without needing to manage the zeros and ones ourselves. For that, we must use efficient algorithms and handle corner cases.
