## Definition
Used relay - electromechanical switches

## Details
- Those switches control current flow by a control wire
- works like a water faucet, just like flow of water can be controlled by opening the faucet or closing it, current flow can be controlled by adding a small current flow to the control wire, which then emits electromagnetic signal, which attracts the other wire and shuts the gate (the wire flowing the main current)

	***Disadvantages*
	- It was slow
	- As it was mechanical, tear and wear happen over time
	- Also due to huge amount of heat production, it attracted insects

- One of the largest computer built with relay based switches is Harvard Mark I, built in 1944.
- It was used in WWII in Manhattan project.
- Harvard Mark II once attracted a large moth. Since then, 'bug' is used to express computer errors
