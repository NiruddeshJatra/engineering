
#story

## Definition
First commercial mechanical computing machine

## Details
- It worked like an odometer. 
- It had 10-teeth gear system, each teeth representing 1,2,3,etc. Inputs could be given be switching nobs, like we do in any nob-based machines. 
- When the first gear completes full cycle, it moves the second gear to motion - this representing multiple digits.
- It can do basic arithmetic operations


## Why it matters to me as a dev
Every invention makes development and computing easier. Development should also follow incremental improvement, not all at once.
