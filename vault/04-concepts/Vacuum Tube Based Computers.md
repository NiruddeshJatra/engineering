## Definition
Used vacuum tubes

## Details
- two electrodes inside a glass chamber. One gets heated, emits electron. If the outside of the glass chamber is positively charged, the emitted electrons get attracted to the other electrode, thus, starting electron flow. This process is called thermionic emission.
- A third electrode worked as a control wire for making the environment outside glass chamber positive or negative.

	***Disadvantages*
	- It was slow
	- As it was mechanical, tear and wear happen over time
	- Also due to huge amount of heat production, it attracted insects

- Collosum Mark I is the first large-scale computer made by vacuum tubes. It was also world's first programmable computer, used in WWII to decrypt Nazi communications.
- Alan Turing is called the father of Computer Science, who created a similar machine for Germans called Bombe.
- ENIAC was world's first general purpose, programmable, commercially used electronic computer.
