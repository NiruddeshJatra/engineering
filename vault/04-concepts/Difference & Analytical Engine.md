## Definition
Next progression of [[Step reckoner]] designed by Charles Babbage - the father of Computing

## Details

- Analytical Engine: General Purpose Engine. Never fully constructed.
- Both are mechanical machines.
- Ada Lovelace wrote Hypothetical instructions for the analytical engine - first written [[programs]]
